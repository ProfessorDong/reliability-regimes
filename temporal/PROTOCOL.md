# Temporal replication in four additional target families

Analysis specification fixed on November 19, 2026, before inspecting the new temporal outcomes.

The targets are human FAAH (CHEMBL2243, fatty-acid amide hydrolase), PTGS2 (CHEMBL230, cyclooxygenase-2), KDR (CHEMBL279, VEGFR2 receptor tyrosine kinase), and KCNH2 (CHEMBL240, hERG potassium channel). These span additional enzyme and channel families represented in the existing structural validation panel. Target choice uses target class only. This is a separate dated cohort retrieved directly from the ChEMBL 37 activity service. A molecule can occur in both the structural and temporal cohorts. The two analyses for a target are not independent replications.

Retrieve every record for the specified target with exact equality, nanomolar units, and standard types IC50, Ki, Kd or EC50, ordered by activity ID. Check pagination, target identity and unique activity IDs, and retain human-target records with a positive finite value and parsable structure. Save activity IDs, document and assay IDs, document year and parent identity and structure. Standardize using RDKit Normalizer, LargestFragmentChooser, Uncharger, sanitization and standard InChIKey. Aggregate within each target. An earlier parent has a known publication year before 2015. Its label is the median pActivity from its dated pre-2015 records only. A later parent has all eligible records dated and first reported from 2015 onward. Its label is the median pActivity across its records. Dates describe publication of eligible activity records for this target.

Include a target if it has at least 100 eligible earlier parents and 50 later parents. Uniformly draw up to 1,250 parents from each era without replacement by sorting on a SHA-256 hash of target and parent InChIKey (no use of activity). This fixed cap limits fitting costs and the potential influence of very large series. This estimand concerns the sampled panel, conditional on the specified cap. Split the earlier sample at random into calibration (one quarter, minimum 50) and proper training. For each target construct 32 random controls drawn from the combined sampled pool with identical training, calibration and test counts, leaving each parent's label unchanged. Distinct seeds 100 through 131 define the controls. Report means and ranges over the controls. These control counts support descriptive effects and do not support a Monte Carlo significance claim below 1/33.

Fit a 300-tree scikit-learn random forest to radius-2, 1,024-bit Morgan fingerprints with minimum leaf size 2, using default all-feature regression splits and seed 0. Compute temporal and random-control RMSE, the Spearman correlation between per-tree prediction standard deviation and absolute error, and empirical coverage of past-calibrated, disagreement-scaled nominal 90% intervals (add 0.001 to each standard deviation). Resample whole Bemis–Murcko scaffolds of the temporal test set 2,000 times, holding the fitted model and calibration quantile fixed, for percentile intervals on temporal metrics. Pool no records across targets for the comparisons. Record each target's outcome regardless of magnitude or sign. No replacement targets, cutoff selection, architecture comparison or exclusion based on a result.

A secondary test asks whether the seed-novelty result is present on later chemistry. For 100 draws of ten historical proper-training parents with pActivity at least 6.5, compute each later compound's distance to its nearest seed and its distance to its nearest proper-training neighbor. Retain the most seed-distant third and divide it into five equal-rank bins of seed distance. Within each bin pair the ordered lower half of training distances with the reversed upper half, dropping pairs tied on training distance and the middle observation in an odd-sized bin. This gives equally many nearer and farther compounds in each narrow novelty bin. Break ties in seed distance by parent hash independently of activity. For each draw compute pooled RMSE in the two groups, farther minus nearer, and report the median difference, its range across the middle 95% of seed draws, the fraction of positive differences, the number of pairs and the mean absolute within-pair difference in seed distance. These intervals describe variation with seed choice on fixed test molecules. If a target has fewer than ten eligible historical actives, report it as ineligible for this secondary analysis.
